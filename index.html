    Or drag the board in the 3D view. Floor or Height above the view picks which way it goes; hold Shift mid-drag to switch and let go to switch back. With Snap on it sits flush against other boards or on a 10 mm grid; hold Alt to do the opposite. Turn, Tip and Roll rotate it 90° (keys R, T, L while the 3D view, its buttons or the parts list has focus). Arrow keys nudge it 10 mm (Shift: 1 mm); Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ move it back and forth.

    Locked: it can't be moved, rotated, resized or deleted until you unlock it. Its name and wood can still change.
