# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-12_

Woodworking planner is a web app to plan a woodworking project and see it in 3D. This policy
describes what it keeps and where. It is short because the app does very little with your data.

## The app collects nothing

There is no account, no online server, no analytics and no advertising. The app makes no network requests
of its own and sends nothing anywhere.

## What it keeps, and where

- **Your project, in your browser.** The app saves the project you are working on in the browser's
  local storage, on your computer, so it is still there when you come back.
- **A copy of a project that could not be opened, in your browser.** If the project saved in your
  browser cannot be opened, for example because a newer version of the app saved it, the app copies it
  under a separate key, `woodworking-planner:unreadable-project`, in the same local storage, so it is
  not lost when you carry on. A later project that cannot be opened replaces that copy. It stays until
  you clear the site's data.
- **A few settings, in your browser.** Whether Snap, Potato and Debug are on.
- **Files you save.** When you save a project, your browser downloads it as a `.woodplan.json` file
  to where it saves downloads. The app reads a file only when you open it.

## The things that happen outside the app

1. **Where you run it from.** While the app is in development, you run it from its source on your
   own computer, and it is served only to your own browser. If it is hosted on maksforge.com later,
   loading the page will be a request to that site, like any web page.
2. **A tip, if you choose to leave one.** Buy Me a Coffee handles the payment under its own privacy
   policy.
3. **An issue you choose to open.** It is public on GitHub, under GitHub's privacy policy.
4. **An email you choose to send.** It reaches the support mailbox and is used only to answer you.

## Deleting your data

Clear the site's data in your browser to delete the saved project, any copy of a project that could
not be opened, and the settings. Files you saved
stay where you put them until you delete them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the history of this file is public
in the repository.
